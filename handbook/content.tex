  {\large
    The admin bot sets up and runs your courses on the department's chat server
    \server: course spaces and rooms, signup links for students, exercise groups,
    templates and your own bots. You talk to it in a direct chat in Element, as
    \botid.

    \bigskip
    \iftechnical
    This edition contains the full user guide (Part~I) and describes how the bot works
    inside (Part~II): its architecture, how it talks to Synapse and maubot, the data it
    keeps, and its security model. It is meant for bot admins and maintainers.
    \else
    This handbook explains every command with examples, the usual order of things in a
    semester, and what to do when something doesn't work. A separate technical edition
    describes how the bot works inside.
    \fi\par}
